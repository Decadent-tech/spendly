Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 15, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create a Spendly account, we collect the information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and password. As you use the service, we also store the expense records you log, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to operate the service: authenticating your account, displaying your expenses back to you, and generating summaries and category breakdowns. We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell it to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored in our database and retained for as long as your account remains active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly may rely on third-party infrastructure providers (such as hosting and font services) to operate the app. These providers process data only as needed to deliver their service and do not have access to your expense records.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out through the contact details provided on our website.
